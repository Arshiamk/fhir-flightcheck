# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, constructive, evidence-led, and mindful that healthcare software
can affect vulnerable people. Never share patient data or credentials. Critique
ideas and implementations, not people.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of private
health information, and sustained disruption are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer. The maintainer may remove,
edit, or reject comments, issues, and other contributions that violate this
code, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
